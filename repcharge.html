Your data and privacy

Stays on your iPhone

Everything stays on your iPhone. With iCloud sync on, your workouts and progress photos are also stored in your own iCloud account, which Liquid Impulse Labs cannot access.

Apple Health

RepCharge reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data. It never stores, copies, exports or sends health data.

Reports you choose to send

Crash reports and problem reports go by email, only if you choose to send them. You see what is attached first. They contain only a diagnostic report, the app version, the build, the iOS version and the device model. Never workouts, health data or photos.

Backups and reset

Backups are files you export yourself. Settings › Reset all data clears the app, and offers a backup first.
