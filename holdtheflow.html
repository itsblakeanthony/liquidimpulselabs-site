Hold the Flow

In development for iOS

Water against oil, with electricity in between

Lead a squad of water people holding the path while waves of oil people pour out of a smog covered city. Drag left and right, and your squad fires electric shots on its own.

Liquid and electricity are two things that normally shouldn't mix. Hold the Flow is built on that idea: your army is water, your shots are electricity, and the enemy is oil. Every oil person you stop bursts into petals and leaves flowers behind.

How it plays

  1. Steer to aimStart each level with a small squad. Drag to move, and your squad fires straight ahead.
  2. Grow your squadChoose between gates, free hostages and pick a prize from each pair of crates.
  3. Hold the lineSurvive the swarm, unleash Overcharge, beat the boss at the end of every level and earn up to three stars.

Features

Your squad

Steering is aiming

Drag left and right. Your squad fires straight ahead on its own, so where you stand is what you hit.

Water people that grow

Ten small water people merge into one big one, and ten big ones into a colossus. Big ones fire a fan of bolts, and split back into small ones when they are hit.

Electric shots

Flickering blue and white bolts, with small arcs crackling between your water people. Chain lightning is an upgrade you earn along the way.

On the path

Two gates, one choice

A blue gate gives a plain bonus. A red gate is a quick sum like ×2 + 10 that can double your squad or cost you half of it. Shoot its glowing number to change it, if that helps you.

Crates and prizes

Crates come in pairs and you can only break one. Prizes include a bigger squad, more range, a wider spray and allies to fight beside you.

Hostages, forks and dynamite

Free captured water people before they turn, pick a side when the road forks, and set the oil alight with dynamite.

The fight

A swarm of oil people

Grunts, runners, brutes, splitters that burst into four, spitters that throw oil, and guards holding hostages.

Allies and Overcharge

Call in a Geyser Turret, a Water Golem, a Storm Cloud or a Tidal Wave, and fill the Overcharge meter to strike everything in range with lightning.

Hold the valley

Oil people that slip past poison the valley. Let the Valley bar empty and the level is lost, so every shot counts.

The Water Cycle

The game travels through the Water Cycle. Each region is a different state of water, and your squad changes form to match. At the end of each region you destroy the Source of the oil and free that region's Spring, a giant water spirit that sends you on to the next.

1. River

A valley under attack from a smog covered city. Water people.

2. Rain

Storm clouds and sky islands. Rain people, with faster fire and stronger lightning.

3. Ice

Glaciers. Ice people, slower but tougher, whose shots slow the oil.

4. Steam

Volcanic springs. Steam people, fast but at risk if they stand still.

5. Ocean

The deep sea. Tide people, with huge squads and waves as weapons.

Questions

When is it coming out?

Hold the Flow is in development. We will share more here as it gets closer to release.

Will it need an internet connection?

No. The plan is for the game to open and be fully playable offline. Game Center leaderboards will be optional.

Is there more than the main levels?

The plan includes an Endless mode for each region and a daily challenge that is the same for every player in a region that day.

Hold the Flow is a working title, and the details on this page may change as development continues.
