Privacy policy

RepCharge, by Liquid Impulse Labs
Effective date: [To be added]

This privacy policy explains how Liquid Impulse Labs ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the RepCharge app ("the app") or visit liquidimpulselabs.com ("the website"). Liquid Impulse Labs is the data controller for the personal data it describes. If you have any questions, email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com.

Summary of key points

  • What personal information do we process? Only what you choose to send us by email, such as a question, a problem report or a crash report. Your training data stays on your iPhone, and we never receive it.
  • Do we process sensitive personal information? No. The app reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data, but health data never leaves your iPhone and never reaches us.
  • Do we receive information from third parties? No.
  • Do we use cookies, analytics or tracking? No, in the app or on the website. Version 1.0 has no accounts, no adverts and no purchases.
  • Do we sell your information or use it for advertising? No.
  • What are your rights? Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and more. See section 15.
  • How do you use your rights? Email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com.

1. What information we collect

In the app. The app keeps the information you enter, such as your workouts, training plans, notes, progress photos and settings. It is stored in the app on your iPhone. We do not receive a copy.

Information you send us. When you email us, or choose to send a crash report or a problem report, we receive your email address, what you write and anything attached. Section 7 explains exactly what a report contains.

The website. When you email us from the website, we receive your message and your email address. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • reply to you and give you support
  • investigate and fix problems you report
  • keep the website secure, through our hosting provider
  • meet our legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

3. Our legal basis for using your information

UK data protection law says we must have a legal basis for using personal data. We rely on:

  • Legitimate interests: to reply to your emails, give you support, fix problems you report and keep the website secure.
  • Consent: when you choose to send us a crash report or a problem report. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete it.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

Apple Health data is read only on your iPhone, with the permission you give in iOS, and it never reaches us.

4. Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data.

  • The app never stores, copies, exports or sends health data. It is not saved in the app's data, in iCloud, in backups or in reports.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never shared with anyone.

You can change what the app can read at any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › RepCharge.

5. Progress photos and iCloud sync

Photos you add are kept in the app on your iPhone. iCloud sync is optional. When it is on, your workouts and progress photos are also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple, so they are the same on each of your devices. Apple provides iCloud under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access your iCloud data. Health data is never part of it.

6. Backups

Backups are files you export yourself from the app. You choose where the file is saved, such as Files or iCloud Drive, and we never receive it. Because the app never stores health data, a backup does not contain it. Backups of your whole iPhone made by Apple follow your Apple settings.

7. Crash reports and Report a problem

Sending a report is always your choice, and it is done by email.

  • If the app closes unexpectedly, it asks the next time you open it whether you would like to send a crash report. If you say yes, it prepares an email to bugs@liquidimpulselabs.com.
  • Settings › Report a problem prepares an email to support@liquidimpulselabs.com, with space for you to describe the problem.

Before anything is sent, you see exactly what is attached. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself from your email app. A report contains only:

  • a diagnostic report
  • the app version and the build
  • the iOS version and the device model

A report never contains your workouts, health data or photos. Anything you choose to write in the email is included too.

8. When and with whom we share information

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the website and receive email (GitHub and our email provider), or where the law requires it. If Liquid Impulse Labs is ever sold or merged with another business, the personal data we hold may pass to the new owner, who must keep using it in line with this policy.

9. Cookies and tracking

The app has no tracking, adverts, analytics or accounts in version 1.0, and no purchases. The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Because we do not track you, there is nothing for your browser's Do Not Track setting to switch off. If any of this changes in a future version, we will update this policy and tell you.

10. International transfers

Apple, GitHub and our email provider may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved for these transfers.

11. How long we keep information
  • The app's data stays on your devices, and in your iCloud account if sync is on, until you delete it.
  • Emails and reports you send us are deleted within 12 months.

12. How to review, update or delete your data
  • You can review and update the data in the app directly, because it is on your own iPhone.
  • To delete it, go to Settings › Reset all data in the app. It offers to export a backup first. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.
  • If iCloud sync is on, Reset all data removes the iCloud copy too. If you used sync before but it is off now, you can delete the iCloud copy in the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Storage › RepCharge.
  • To ask us to delete emails or reports you have sent us, email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com.

13. How we keep information safe

The app's data is protected by your iPhone's own security, so keep a passcode on your iPhone and keep iOS up to date. We do not run servers that store your app data. Emails you send us pass through email providers, and no method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not include anything sensitive in a report or an email.

14. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

15. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy. You can also withdraw any consent you have given. As the app's data is on your own devices, you control it directly. The only personal data we hold is what you email us.

To make a request, email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com. We may need to check your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within one month, and there is normally no charge. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

16. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app or on the website if a change is significant.
