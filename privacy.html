Privacy policy

PPL Training, by Liquid Impulse Labs
Effective date: [To be added]

Liquid Impulse Labs ("we", "us") makes the PPL Training app ("the app") and runs liquidimpulselabs.com. This policy explains what the app and the website do with your information.

The short version

  • Your training data stays on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also kept in your own private iCloud account.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • We cannot see your workouts, measurements or progress photos.
  • The app reads Apple Health on your iPhone only to show your workout data, and never stores, copies, exports or sends it.
  • The only time anything from the app reaches us is when you choose to email us a crash report or a problem report, after seeing exactly what it contains.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

The app keeps the information you enter: workouts, sets, weights, reps and reps in reserve, notes, your training plan and its history, your routine, rest days and holidays, weekly reviews, body measurements, progress photos and settings. It is stored inside the app on your iPhone. We do not receive a copy.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads workouts, heart rate and active energy from Apple Health on your iPhone. It uses them for one thing: to show the duration, average and maximum heart rate and calories of the Apple Watch workout that matches a session you logged.

  • The app reads these values when you look at a session, and keeps them in memory only while the app is open.
  • The app never stores, copies, exports or sends Apple Health data. It is not saved in the app's data, in iCloud, in backups or in reports.
  • The app never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never shared with anyone.

You can change what the app can read at any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › PPL Training, or in the Settings app › Health › Data Access and Devices.

Progress photos

Photos you add are kept inside the app on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also kept in your own private iCloud account, so your other devices have them. They are included in backups you export. We cannot see them.

iCloud sync (optional)

When iCloud sync is on, the app's data (never Apple Health data) is kept in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so it is the same on each of your devices. Apple provides this under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access it. Using Reset all data while sync is on removes the iCloud copy too.

Backups

Export backup creates one file with the app's data, including progress photos. It never includes Apple Health data. You choose where the file goes (for example Files or iCloud Drive), and we never receive it. Backups of your whole iPhone, made by iCloud Backup or a computer, follow Apple's settings.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, rest over notifications and the workout Live Activity are created on your iPhone. Nothing about them is sent to us. The app asks for permission to send notifications the first time you start a rest timer or turn on reminders, never when it first opens.

Crash reports and Report a problem

The app uses Apple's MetricKit to collect crash and hang diagnostics on your iPhone.

  • If the app closes unexpectedly, it asks the next time you open it whether you would like to send a report. If you say yes, it prepares an email to bugs@liquidimpulselabs.com.
  • Settings › Report a problem prepares an email to support@liquidimpulselabs.com, with space for you to describe the problem.

Before anything is sent, you see exactly what is attached and can open each item. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself from your email app. A report contains:

  • the technical diagnostic from iOS: what the app was doing when it crashed or stopped responding, the app and iOS versions, the device type and the region format
  • the app version and build, the iOS version and the device model
  • anything you write

A report never contains your workouts, measurements, Apple Health data or photos. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems, and we delete it within 12 months.

Purchases

This version of the app is free and has no purchases.

The website

When you email us from the website, we receive your message and email address. The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the website and receive email (GitHub and our email provider), or where the law requires it. Apple, GitHub and our email provider may process data outside the UK, using approved legal safeguards.

How long data is kept

The app's data stays on your devices, and in your iCloud account if sync is on, until you delete it with Settings › Reset all data or by deleting the app. Emails and reports you send us are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy. As the app's data is on your own devices, you control it directly; the only personal data we hold is what you email us. To make a request, email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

The app's data is protected by your iPhone's own security. Keep a passcode on your iPhone and keep iOS up to date.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app or on the website if a change is significant.
