Privacy policy

RepCharge, by Liquid Impulse Labs
Effective date: [To be added]

Liquid Impulse Labs ("we", "us") makes the RepCharge app ("the app") and runs liquidimpulselabs.com ("the website"). This policy explains what the app and the website do with your information. We are responsible for the personal data it describes.

The short version

  • Your training data stays on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, your workouts and progress photos are also stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access.
  • Version 1.0 has no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no purchases, and we never sell data.
  • We cannot see your workouts, training plans or progress photos.
  • The app reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data, and never stores, copies, exports or sends health data.
  • The only time anything from the app reaches us is when you choose to email us a crash report or a problem report, after seeing exactly what is attached.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

The app keeps the information you enter, such as your workouts, training plans, notes, progress photos and settings. It is stored in the app on your iPhone. We do not receive a copy.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data.

  • The app never stores, copies, exports or sends health data. It is not saved in the app's data, in iCloud, in backups or in reports.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never shared with anyone.

You can change what the app can read at any time in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › RepCharge.

Progress photos and iCloud sync

Photos you add are kept in the app on your iPhone. iCloud sync is optional. When it is on, your workouts and progress photos are also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple, so they are the same on each of your devices. Apple provides iCloud under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access your iCloud data. Health data is never part of it.

Backups

Backups are files you export yourself from the app. You choose where the file is saved, such as Files or iCloud Drive, and we never receive it. Because the app never stores health data, a backup does not contain it. Backups of your whole iPhone made by Apple follow your Apple settings.

Crash reports and Report a problem

Sending a report is always your choice, and it is done by email.

  • If the app closes unexpectedly, it asks the next time you open it whether you would like to send a crash report. If you say yes, it prepares an email to bugs@liquidimpulselabs.com.
  • Settings › Report a problem prepares an email to support@liquidimpulselabs.com, with space for you to describe the problem.

Before anything is sent, you see exactly what is attached. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself from your email app. A report contains only:

  • a diagnostic report
  • the app version and the build
  • the iOS version and the device model

A report never contains your workouts, health data or photos. Anything you choose to write in the email is included too. When you email us, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems, and we delete it within 12 months.

No tracking, adverts, accounts or purchases

Version 1.0 has no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no purchases. If that changes in a future version, we will update this policy and tell you.

The website

When you email us from the website, we receive your message and your email address. The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the website and receive email (GitHub and our email provider), or where the law requires it. Apple, GitHub and our email provider may process data outside the UK, using approved legal safeguards.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • The app's data stays on your devices, and in your iCloud account if sync is on, until you delete it.
  • To delete it, go to Settings › Reset all data in the app. It offers to export a backup first. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.
  • If iCloud sync is on, Reset all data removes the iCloud copy too. If you used sync before but it is off now, you can delete the iCloud copy in the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Storage › RepCharge.
  • Emails and reports you send us are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy. You can also withdraw any consent you have given. As the app's data is on your own devices, you control it directly. The only personal data we hold is what you email us. To make a request, email privacy@liquidimpulselabs.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

The app's data is protected by your iPhone's own security. Keep a passcode on your iPhone and keep iOS up to date.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app or on the website if a change is significant.
