Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

You can restore previous purchases from within the app.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies.

How do I back up my data?

Your data is stored on your device. Use the app's export feature or turn on iCloud Sync to keep a backup. We cannot recover data that is lost if you delete the app, lose your device or reset it.

How do I change Apple Health access?

Go to Settings, then Health, then Data Access and Devices. You can change or withdraw permission at any time.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on your device. If you used iCloud Sync, also delete the iCloud copy in your iCloud storage settings.
