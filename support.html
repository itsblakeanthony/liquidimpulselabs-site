Support

RepCharge, by Liquid Impulse Labs

Contact support

Email support@liquidimpulselabs.com. The quickest way is Settings › Report a problem in the app. It prepares an email with a diagnostic report, the app version, the build, the iOS version and the device model attached, so we can help sooner. You see what is attached before anything is sent.

Report a bug or a crash

  • In the app, go to Settings › Report a problem, describe what happened and what you were doing, and send the email.
  • If the app closes unexpectedly, the next time you open it, it asks whether to send a crash report to bugs@liquidimpulselabs.com. You see exactly what is attached first.
  • Reports never include your workouts, health data or photos, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
  • If Mail is not set up on your iPhone, the app lets you share the report with another app or copy it, so you can email it to us.

Importing a plan

RepCharge works with any training split. To bring in your own plan, import it as text in Settings › Plans. The app checks the text and previews the change before applying it. Here is a short example of plan text:

Day 1 Upper
Bench press 3 x 8 to 10
Barbell row 3 x 8 to 10
Overhead press 3 x 10 to 12

Day 2 Lower
Squat 3 x 6 to 8
Romanian deadlift 3 x 8 to 10
Calf raise 3 x 12 to 15

Change the days, exercises, sets and reps to match your own plan. RepCharge also includes a demo plan, labelled DEMO in the app. It is for illustration only and is not advice tailored to you.

Backing up and restoring

  • Back up: Settings › Backup › Export backup saves one file with the data in the app. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. Backups are files you export yourself, and we never receive them. Health data is never in a backup, because the app never stores it.
  • Restore: Settings › Backup › Import backup, then choose the file. The app shows what the backup holds, then you choose Replace everything (the app becomes exactly the backup) or Merge (what is in the backup and not yet in the app is added).
  • iCloud sync is not a backup: a workout deleted on one device is deleted everywhere. Keep exporting backups.

Reset all data

Settings › Reset all data clears everything in the app. It offers to export a backup first.

  • If iCloud sync is on, the copy in your iCloud account is removed too, along with the data on your other devices that sync with it.
  • If sync is off but you used it before, the iCloud copy is not touched. To remove it, open the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Storage › RepCharge, and delete its data.

Apple Health

RepCharge reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data. It never stores, copies, exports or sends health data. To change or remove access, open the Health app › Sharing › Apps › RepCharge.

iPhone only

RepCharge is for iPhone only. Running it on an iPad is not supported or guaranteed.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Everything stays on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, your workouts and progress photos are also stored in your own iCloud account, which Liquid Impulse Labs cannot access. Backups are files you export yourself.

Does RepCharge keep my health data? No. The app reads Apple Health on your iPhone to show workout data, and never stores, copies, exports or sends it.

Does RepCharge work offline? Yes.
