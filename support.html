Support

PPL Training, by Liquid Impulse Labs

Contact support

Email support@liquidimpulselabs.com. The quickest way is Settings › Report a problem in the app: it prepares an email with the app version, iOS version and device model attached, so we can help sooner.

Report a bug

  • In the app, go to Settings › Report a problem, describe what happened and what you were doing, and send the email. Recent crash and hang diagnostics are attached; you can open each one, or leave it out, before sending.
  • If the app closes unexpectedly, the next time you open it, it asks whether to send a crash report to bugs@liquidimpulselabs.com. You see exactly what is attached first.
  • Reports never include your workouts, measurements, Apple Health data or photos, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.
  • If Mail is not set up on your iPhone, the app lets you share the report with another app or copy it, so you can email it to us.

iPhone only

PPL Training is an iPhone only app. Running it on an iPad is not supported or guaranteed.

Backing up and restoring

  • Back up: Settings › Backup › Export backup saves one file with everything in the app: workouts and sets, your plan and its history, weights, rest days, holidays, weekly reviews, body measurements, progress photos and settings. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. Apple Health data is never in a backup, because the app never stores it.
  • Restore: Settings › Backup › Import backup, then choose the file. The app shows what the backup holds, then you choose Replace everything (the app becomes exactly the backup) or Merge (your current plan, weights and settings stay, and any workouts, rest days, holidays, reviews, measurements, photos and older plan versions that are not in the app yet are added).
  • The app reminds you after 30 days without a backup.
  • iCloud sync is not a backup: a workout deleted on one device is deleted everywhere. Keep exporting backups.

Moving to a new iPhone

Export a backup on your old iPhone, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Import backup on the new one. If iCloud sync is on, your data also arrives on the new iPhone once it is signed in to the same iCloud account and sync is turned on there.

Resetting the app

Settings › Reset all data clears everything in the app and starts again with the demo plan at Stage 1. It offers to export a backup first and asks you to confirm twice.

  • If iCloud sync is on, the copy in your iCloud account is removed too, along with the data on your other devices that sync with it.
  • If sync is off but you used it before, the iCloud copy is not touched. To remove it, open the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Storage › PPL Training, and delete its data.

Apple Health

  • Connect Apple Health in the app under Settings › Apple Health, or from a workout summary. The app only reads workouts, heart rate and active energy, on your iPhone, to show them with your sessions. It never stores, copies, exports or sends Health data, and never writes to Health.
  • To change access: the Health app › Sharing › Apps › PPL Training (or the Settings app › Health › Data Access and Devices).
  • If no Apple Watch data appears for a session, check that Workouts, Heart Rate and Active Energy are allowed, and that the watch workout was recorded at the same time as the session, then tap Check again.

Notifications

The app asks for permission the first time you start a rest timer or turn on reminders. To change it later, open the Settings app › Notifications › PPL Training. Reminder times are in the app under Settings › Reminders.

The demo plan

The built in plan is a demo, marked DEMO in the app. It is for illustration only and is not advice tailored to you. Import your own plan any time in Settings › Plans.

Purchases
