PPL Training

Gym Training Companion

Follow a plan that progresses, log sets in a tap and level up when you're ready. Your data stays on your iPhone.

How it works

  1. Follow the planUse the built-in plan or paste in your own.
  2. Log sets in a tapThe rest timer starts as soon as you log a set.
  3. Level up when you're readyWhen every set hits the top of the rep range, you'll see it's time to level up.

Features

Training

A plan that progresses

Four stages, each with its own milestones and a lighter deload week. After the last stage, the plan loops back to a stage you choose, or repeats the last one.

Bring your own plan

Paste a plan as plain text. The app checks it, explains what it read, lets you choose where it goes and previews the change before applying it, with Undo and full version history.

Clear progression

“Level up” when every set hits the top of the rep range. Weights never change on their own.

Rest timer

Starts when you log a set, with longer rests for heavy lifts. Your rest timer is on your lock screen, where you can log a set or skip rest without unlocking.

In the gym

A plate calculator, one-tap exercise swaps when a machine is taken, and a pain flag that suggests a safer alternative.

Plain English

Every training term has a help icon, and there's a full glossary.

Tracking

Log every session

Record workouts, sets, weights, repetitions and notes.

Weekly review

On your rest day, see sets per muscle, sessions done, personal records and what's ready to level up.

A streak that forgives life

A weekly target (5 of 6 by default). Holiday mode pauses the streak and your plan, and a welcome-back option eases you in after a break.

History

Tap any day for the full session, with personal records, swaps, notes and Apple Watch data.

Body progress

Keep body measurements and progress photos alongside your training.

Reads from Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads workouts, heart rate and active energy from Apple Health, usually recorded by your Apple Watch. It never writes to Health.

Reminders

Reminders are created on your device, so nothing about them is sent to us.

Privacy and data

Private by default

No accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your training data stays on your iPhone.

Optional iCloud Sync

Turn on iCloud Sync to keep your data in your own private iCloud account.

Export your data

Export a backup file and choose where it is saved.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. If you have one, the app can read your workouts, heart rate and active energy from Apple Health and show them next to your sessions.

Can I use my own programme?

Yes. Paste it in as plain text. The app checks it, shows you what it read and previews the change before applying it.

What if I miss a day?

The rotation continues.
