PPL Training

Push Pull Legs training log

A push, pull, legs training log for iPhone that keeps your training on your phone.

How it works

  1. Follow the planTry the demo plan, or paste in your own.
  2. Log sets in a tapBig, one handed controls, and a rest timer that keeps going on your lock screen.
  3. Level up when you're readyAdd reps, then weight. When every set hits the top of the rep range, you'll see it's time to level up.

Features

Training

Log every set

Big, one handed controls, with reps in reserve, warm up sets, swaps and notes.

Rest timer

Keeps going on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, with Set done and Skip rest buttons.

Double progression

Add reps, then weight. A Level up button appears when every set hits the top of the rep range. Weights never change on their own.

A plan that progresses

Stages, each with its own milestones and a lighter deload week. After the last stage, the plan loops back to a stage you choose, or repeats the last one.

Bring your own plan

Paste a plan as plain text. The app checks it, explains what it read, lets you choose where it goes and previews the change before applying it, with Undo and full version history.

In the gym

A plate calculator, one-tap exercise swaps when a machine is taken, and a pain flag that suggests a safer alternative.

Exercise Library and training guide

Compiled from published research, with every source listed in the app. General information, not tailored to you.

Plain English

Every training term has a help icon, and there's a full glossary.

Tracking

Weekly review and charts

Weekly sets per muscle against your stage's targets, a weekly review, personal records and charts.

A streak that forgives life

A weekly target (5 of 6 by default). Holiday mode pauses the streak and your plan, and a welcome-back option eases you in after a break.

History

Tap any day for the full session, with personal records, swaps and notes.

Body progress

Body measurements and progress photos with side by side compare.

Apple Health (optional)

See your Apple Watch heart rate, calories and duration with each session. The app only reads Health, and never stores or shares it.

Reminders

Reminders are created on your iPhone, so nothing about them is sent to us.

Privacy and data

Private by default

No account, no adverts, no tracking. Your training data stays on your iPhone.

Backups you control

Export one backup file and choose where it is saved. We never receive it.

Optional iCloud sync

Keep the same data on each of your devices through your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Your data is yours No account No adverts No tracking Your data stays on your iPhone

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. This version of the app is free and has no purchases.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. If you have one and you allow it, the app can show the heart rate, calories and duration of your Apple Watch workout next to the session you logged.

Can I use my own programme?

Yes. Paste it in as plain text. The app checks it, shows you what it read and previews the change before applying it.

What if I miss a day?

The rotation continues.

Does it work on iPad?

PPL Training is an iPhone only app. Running it on an iPad is not supported or guaranteed.

The training information and the demo plan are general information, not medical or personal training advice. Check with a doctor or physiotherapist before starting if you have an injury or a health condition. See our Terms of Use.
